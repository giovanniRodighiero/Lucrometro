Auto-refresh on the details screen

Three signals, each with one job. Quotes are about 15 minutes delayed, so this is "check again", not a live feed.

Open Market state: filled dot Open, hollow dot Closed, neutral colours. Tap it for the details sheet. The control: tonal fill when on, plain when off, 38% when disabled. Tap to start or stop. Halo on the chart's last point: polling is running. Only while on. It takes the line's colour (gain, loss, neutral).

Rules

  • Starts off every time the screen opens.
  • Polls every 30 s, counted from the end of the previous fetch. One request at a time.
  • Stops when you tap the icon, leave the screen or the app, the market closes, or a fetch fails (no retries; the header goes stale).
  • Market closed: icon disabled, no halo, the stamp names the session day.
  • The sheet shows the session hours, last quote, delay and the auto-refresh state. A foreign exchange shows both clocks there (decision 0032).
